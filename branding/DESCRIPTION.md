# Retrofit

**Play 1.7.10 Forge mods in Minecraft 1.20.1.**

Retrofit runs a real Minecraft 1.7.10 with Forge 10.13.4 inside your 1.20.1 game and connects it
to the 1.20.1 world. Your 1.7.10 mods run their own code, so their blocks, items, machines, GUIs,
creatures, recipes, world generation and sounds work as they were made, alongside your 1.20.1
mods.

> **Beta.** The tested mods below work; others are untested. Keep backups of worlds you care
> about, and please report what breaks.

## Getting started

1. Install **Forge 1.20.1** (47.x) and put Retrofit in your `mods` folder.
2. Start the game once. Retrofit creates a `retrofit/mods` folder in your game directory.
3. Put your **1.7.10 Forge mod jars** in `retrofit/mods` and start again.

On first start Retrofit downloads Minecraft 1.7.10 and Forge 10.13.4.1614 from Mojang and Forge
(skipped if your launcher already has them). Retrofit itself contains no Mojang code and no mods.
Every start spends about 5-10 seconds booting 1.7.10 while mods load.

**Servers** work the same way: install it on a Forge 1.20.1 server and put the 1.7.10 mods in the
server's `retrofit/mods`. Players need the same 1.7.10 mods, and the same configs from
`retrofit/config` (as in any 1.7.10 pack).

## What works

- **Blocks**: textures, custom models and tile entity renderers, collision, tools and tiers,
  redstone, right-click, placing, breaking and the mods' own drops
- **Machines and storage**: ticking tile entities, saving, client sync, 1.20.1 hoppers and
  pipes moving items in and out, and legacy pipes and machines using vanilla chests and furnaces
- **Items, tools, weapons, food and armor**, with the mods' own behaviour
- **GUIs**: the mods' own screens and containers, buttons, progress bars, shift-click
- **Creatures**: the mods' mobs with their own AI and models, spawn eggs, natural spawning and
  riding (brooms, mounts, sleds)
- **Dimensions**: the mods' own dimensions, generated by the mods (Thaumcraft's Outer Lands,
  Witchery's Spirit World, Mo' Creatures' Wyvern Lair)
- **Players**: mod potions, flight, the mods' saved player data, living events, tooltips
- **Chunk loading** through the mods' chunk loading tickets
- **Recipes**: all crafting recipes, furnace recipes, fuels
- **World generation**: mod ores, plants and structures in new chunks
- **Commands, key bindings, HUD overlays, sounds, mod packets**
- **Multiplayer**: dedicated servers (LAN not tested yet)
- **With 1.20.1 mods**: energy (RF and EU <-> Forge Energy), items, fluids and tanks, the Ore
  Dictionary as tags, creatures that fight each other, recipes in JEI, REI and EMI, villager
  trades, and wood types that Every Compat and other Moonlight-based mods pick up
- **The full world height**: legacy ores generate below y=0 too, and legacy creatures path find
  down there
- **Looks**: items in hand and in inventories placed as in 1.7.10 (OreSpawn's giant swords too),
  biome-coloured leaves and grass, coloured items, mods' glowing effects, mods' own skies, and
  `RenderPlayerEvent` for capes, wings and transformations
- **Performance**: legacy machines that never tick cost nothing; a heavy pack runs at about
  10 ms per server tick

## Tested mods

Each of these was tested in a new world (every block, machine, creature and item, plus world
generation), and all of them together in singleplayer and on a dedicated server:

- **Works:** Thaumcraft, Witchery, Ars Magica 2, BuildCraft, BiblioCraft, Iron Chests, OreSpawn,
  Mo' Creatures, RotaryCraft (with DragonAPI), Tinkers' Construct, Pam's HarvestCraft,
  Carpenter's Blocks, Chisel, IndustrialCraft 2, GregTech 5 Unofficial, Lycanite's Mobs
- **Loads:** CodeChickenCore and NEI (NEI's panel only on 1.7.10 screens; use JEI)

Libraries they need (Baubles, DragonAPI, AnimationAPI, Mantle, CustomMobSpawner) go in
`retrofit/mods` too. Other mods are untested; reports are very welcome.

## Not yet

- Player render events for other players
- Recipes of mods' own recipe classes (IC2's, Tinkers' tools) in the recipe book and JEI (they
  still craft)
- Mods' own cloud and weather renderers

## Known issues

- Connecting to a server right after the game starts can occasionally hang at "Logging in...".
  Cancel and connect again.
- RotaryCraft/DragonAPI on a dedicated server: add the JVM argument
  `-DragonAPI_silence_ASM_POTIONPACKETID1`.
- 1.7.10 configs are in `retrofit/config`; ID clashes between mods (e.g. Witchery and RotaryCraft
  potion IDs) are fixed there, as in a 1.7.10 pack.

## Requirements

Forge 1.20.1 (47.x), Java 17 or newer (tested with 17, 21 and 26). Internet on first start, unless the Minecraft launcher already has
1.7.10 and Forge 10.13.4.1614 installed.
